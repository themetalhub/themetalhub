Refund & Return Policy - THE METAL HUB

Effective Date: 12 Oct 2026

1. No Return for Cut Material

Important: MS Plates, Pipes, Angles, Channels that are cut as per customer requirement cannot be returned or refunded. Please confirm size before ordering.

2. Manufacturing Defects

For Wheel Hub / Custom Fabrication items: If manufacturing defect found within 24 hours of delivery, share photos/video on WhatsApp. We will replace or re-manufacture at no cost. No cash refund for job work.

3. Wrong Material / Shortage

If we deliver wrong grade or short weight, inform within 24 hours with weighbridge slip & photos. After verification, we will deliver balance or refund difference to bank/UPI within 3-5 working days.

4. Order Cancellation

Bulk stock order can be cancelled before cutting/loading. After cutting/loading, 15% cutting charges will be deducted. Custom fabrication job work once started cannot be cancelled.

5. Scrap & Market Rate Items

Metal Scrap is sold on "as-is, where-is" basis and at daily market rate. No return / refund applicable.

6. Refund Mode

All approved refunds will be made via Bank Transfer / UPI to original payment account only, within 5 working days after approval. No cash refund.
